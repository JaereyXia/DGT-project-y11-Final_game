Who We Are


At cafe, we’re more than just a café—we’re a community hub where every cup is crafted with care and every guest is treated like family. Located in [City/Town Name], we pride ourselves on serving high-quality coffee, homemade treats, and warm vibes. If you love connecting with people, geeking out over latte art, and thrive in a team-oriented environment, we’d love to meet you!


Positions Available:


- Barista/Café Team Member (Full-time & Part-time)
- Shift Supervisor (Experience required)


What You’ll Do


- Craft delicious coffee drinks with precision and creativity.
- Provide exceptional, friendly service to every guest.
- Maintain a clean, welcoming café environment.
- Learn about coffee origins, brewing methods, and our menu.
- Work with a fun, supportive team to keep the café buzzing.


We’re Looking For Someone Who


- Loves coffee (or is eager to learn!).
- Has a positive attitude and genuine passion for serving others.
- Can work flexible hours, including mornings, weekends, or holidays.
- Takes pride in cleanliness and attention to detail.
- Communicates clearly and works well in a team.
(Prior experience is a plus but not required—we train the right person!)


Why Work With Us?


- Free drinks during shifts + discounts for friends/family.
- Competitive hourly wage + tips.
- Opportunities to grow (barista training, leadership roles).
- Fun team events, coffee tastings, and volunteer opportunities.
- A supportive, inclusive workplace where you’re valued.
